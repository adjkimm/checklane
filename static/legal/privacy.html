Plain-English privacy

Privacy

Last updated: September 27, 2026.

What we collect

  • The domain you audit. Just the address you type in — we need it to visit the site and run the audit.
  • Your name and email — only when you give them to us. That's the beta waiting list, the services message form, and the "questions first?" box under your audit result. If you don't fill in a form, we don't have them.
  • Your purchase email. When you buy the full report, Stripe tells us the email address on the purchase so we can send your receipt and your report link. Stripe handles the actual payment; we never see, store, or touch your card details.

Why we collect it

  • The domain — to run your audit.
  • Name and email from forms — to reply to you and to invite you when the beta opens.
  • Purchase email — to deliver your report, send your receipt, and handle refunds.

That's it. We don't build profiles, run ad tracking, or do anything else with your details.

Who sends the emails

Our transactional emails (receipts, report links, replies) go out through Resend, our email-sending service. They process the addresses strictly to deliver the mail.

No sale of data

We don't sell, rent, or trade your personal information. Full stop.

How long we keep it

  • Purchase receipts: kept as long as we need them for accounting, tax, and refund records.
  • Form messages: kept as long as needed to reply and follow up. They are also forwarded to our operator's email inbox for handling, where they are retained as business records.
  • Free audit domains: not tied to any identity and not kept as a profile on you.

If you'd like us to delete your details, just ask (see "Your data and deletion requests" below) and we'll delete what we reasonably can. Some records, like accounting and tax receipts, must be kept by law, and backups or logs may retain copies for a limited time.

Your data and deletion requests

This section describes a voluntary practice of ours — not a claim about any particular privacy law.

What's held where:

  • Form messages (name, email, message): sent through Resend and forwarded to our operator's email inbox for handling.
  • Beta waiting list (name, email): stored in our signup log and sent to our operator's email inbox.
  • Purchase email: used for your receipt, your report link, and re-delivery if you lose it. It also appears in our purchase records, which we keep for accounting, tax, and refund purposes.

How to ask: reply to any Checklane email from the address you want deleted, or use the message form on our homepage with that email address. We verify it's you by matching the request to the email address on the records — a deletion request must come from the same address. Requests made through the message form get a confirmation email first; we only proceed once you've replied to it.

What happens: we delete your form messages from our inbox, and beta waiting-list entries are deleted from the inbox and removed from the signup log where we can. Your purchase email stays in our purchase and receipt records — we need it for accounting, tax, refund handling, and report re-delivery (see "How long we keep it" above) — and we keep it and use it only for those purposes. Backups or service logs (Resend, Render) may retain copies for a limited time. There's no automated deletion — each request is handled by a person.

Timing: we respond to deletion requests within 2 business days and send you a confirmation email when it's done.

Contact

Questions, corrections, or deletion requests? Reply to any Checklane email or use the message form on our homepage — a human reads it.
