Refund policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026.

Our guarantee

One promise, no weasel words:

  1. Delivery guarantee. If your paid report fails to generate, can't be displayed, or you can't get to it, we'll re-run it for you or refund you — your choice.

What's not covered

The report is a technical reading of your site at audit time: a score and a fix list, not a promise of sales or AI recommendations. We don't refund purchases because of the score itself or because you disagree with the findings.

How to request a refund

Reply to your Checklane receipt email and say you'd like a refund (or a re-run). That's the whole process. We respond to refund requests within 48 hours. Refunds go back to the card you paid with and typically arrive within 5–10 business days, depending on your bank.

Never received a receipt email? Use the message form on our homepage with the email you used at checkout and we'll look up your purchase.

If the report was delivered, the sale is final — but the delivery guarantee always stands: if we failed to deliver what you paid for, we re-run it for you or refund you, your choice.
