Refund policy

Last updated: September 27, 2026.

Our guarantee

Two promises, no weasel words:

  1. 7-day satisfaction guarantee. If you're not happy with the full report for any reason, tell us within 7 days of your purchase and we'll refund you in full — no questions asked.
  2. Delivery guarantee. If your report fails to generate, or you can't get to it, we'll re-run it for you or refund you — your choice, regardless of the 7-day window.

How to request a refund

Reply to your Checklane receipt email and say you'd like a refund (or a re-run). That's the whole process. Refunds go back to the card you paid with and typically arrive within 5–10 business days, depending on your bank.

After 7 days

If the report was delivered and 7 days have passed, refunds are at our discretion — but the delivery guarantee always stands: if we failed to deliver what you paid for, we make it right.

Contact

Reply to any Checklane email or use the message form on our homepage — a human reads it.
