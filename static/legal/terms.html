What Checklane is

Checklane is a technical audit service for small online stores. You enter a domain, and we check its public pages for the structure AI shoppers need to find, read, and buy from your store. We only ever read public pages — the same ones any visitor can see. No account, no login, no admin access.

The $9 full report

The free audit gives you a 0–100 score. The full report — a one-time digital good, $9 USD — explains every check and ranks every fix for your store. It opens on screen right after you complete checkout (keep your browser tab open until it loads). We'll also email you a receipt with a link to your report — if it doesn't arrive, enter your checkout email at our lost-report page and we'll resend it.

Keep that receipt email — it's your way back to the report if you close the tab. See also our refund and delivery guarantee.

Acceptable use of the audit

  • Run audits on stores you own or have permission to check.
  • Don't hammer the service — automated bulk probing, scraping, or abuse that degrades the audit for others may be rate-limited or blocked.
  • Don't use Checklane's name, badge, or reports to mislead anyone about what was tested (see our attestation wording on the badge itself).

What we don't promise

Checklane grades what's technically verifiable about your store at audit time. A score is a technical reading, not a promise:

  • Not a promise of sales, orders, or revenue.
  • Not a promise that any AI system — ChatGPT, Claude, Google, Perplexity, or anything else — will recommend your products. Recommendations are the AI's call.
  • AI systems, protocols, and the web change constantly; a score from today can be stale tomorrow. Re-run the free audit to re-check.

Payment

Payments are processed by Stripe. We never see or store your card details. The price you see at checkout is the price you're charged.
