What we look at

AI shoppers don’t browse your site, they read its structure. These five checks decide how reliably they can find, read, and buy from you.

The AI checkout handshake

The behind-the-scenes file that tells AI shoppers “yes, you can buy here.” It’s the faster, more reliable machine path for agent checkout. Browser-based AI shoppers can still buy through normal checkout without it, but the handshake makes it consistent.

Product info AI can read

Clean, labeled details: names, photos, prices, stock. AI reads labels first; unlabeled pages may as well be invisible.

Can AI find your products?

Your sitemap and product feeds. If AI can’t list your catalog, it can’t sell a thing.

Are you blocking AI visitors?

We visit your site as nine AI crawlers and fetchers. If your security turns them away, you may be invisible to the AI systems that shop for people, though blocking a training crawler doesn’t block AI shopping itself.

Can AI read your pages?

The basics: real text (not just JavaScript), readable pages, and no accidental “keep out” signs.

My site isn’t a store. Is this for me?

Yes. Checklane grades any public website. Storefront-specific checks like product data are marked not applicable for non-storefronts; you’re scored on what matters for your site: AI bot access, readability, llms.txt, sitemap, meta tags, and FAQ content.

Recommendations are the AI’s call, but here’s what matters more: AI can only recommend stores it can read. Most stores are unreliably legible to AI shoppers right now.
