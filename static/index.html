Common questions

Does a good score guarantee sales?

No. We grade what’s technically true about your store — things we can actually check. We don’t certify your business, and nobody can promise what an AI will recommend.

Do you need my password or admin access?

Nope. We only read your public pages — the same ones any visitor sees. Nothing to install, no login.

Will this make ChatGPT recommend my products?

Nobody can promise that. What we measure is whether AI can find, read, and buy from your store. Being findable is step one — necessary, but not a guarantee.

How do I get my full fix list?

Run the free audit, then send us a message from your results page. We’ll walk you through every check — ranked by what matters most for your store — personally.
