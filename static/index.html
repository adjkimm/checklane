What the audit checks

UCP capability profile

Whether your store publishes a machine-readable profile under the open Universal Commerce Protocol — the file agents read to discover that you accept agent transactions.

Structured product data

schema.org Product markup: names, images, prices, availability, identifiers. Agents parse structured data first; pages without it are effectively invisible.

Feed discoverability

Sitemaps and machine-readable product feeds. If agents can’t enumerate your catalog, they can’t sell it.

Agent access

We request your homepage as six known AI-agent user agents. If your bot defenses block them, agents can’t see your products at all.

Machine-readability

robots.txt posture toward AI crawlers, server-rendered content, and HTML fundamentals. Agents read HTML, not your JavaScript bundle.

Questions

Is this a certification or a guarantee?

No. The audit is a readiness assessment: it attests to verifiable, machine-readable facts about your store. It does not certify your business, guarantee sales, or promise that any agent will recommend you.

Do you need access to my store?

No. The audit reads only your site’s public pages — the same pages any visitor or agent would see. Nothing is installed, and no login is required.

Will this make ChatGPT recommend my products?

Nobody controls what agents recommend. What we measure is whether agents can discover, read, and transact with your store. Readiness is necessary; it isn’t sufficient — and we’ll always say so.

What does the $29/mo founding beta include?

The agent console: weekly checks of whether your products surface in AI shopping answers, plus detected agent traffic on your own store — the proof that agents are finding you. And the Checklane authentication badge: an earned, verifiable identity credential for your storefront. Cancel anytime.

Who is Checklane?

We’re a small team building the trust layer for agentic commerce — starting with the merchants the big platforms overlook. This audit is our first product.
