What we look at

Your human shopper sees one assistant. Your site sees nine agents: the major AI crawlers and agent fetchers, each reading your site its own way. 59 checks across five categories test whether every one of them can find, read, and buy from you.

The AI checkout handshake

The behind-the-scenes file that tells AI shoppers “yes, you can buy here”: Google’s Universal Commerce Protocol profile (/.well-known/ucp) and Agentic Commerce Protocol (ACP) checkout signals. It’s the faster, more reliable machine path for agent checkout. Browser-based AI shoppers can still buy through normal checkout without it, but the handshake makes it consistent.

Product info AI can read

Clean, labeled details using schema.org Product markup: names, photos, prices, stock. AI reads labels first; unlabeled pages may as well be invisible.

Can AI find your products?

Your sitemap and product feeds. If AI can’t list your catalog, it can’t sell a thing.

Are you blocking AI visitors?

We visit your site as nine AI crawlers and fetchers (GPTBot, ClaudeBot, PerplexityBot, Google’s AI crawlers, and more) and read your robots.txt bot rules. If your security turns them away, you may be invisible to the AI systems that shop for people, though blocking a training crawler doesn’t block AI shopping itself.

Can AI read your pages?

The basics: real text (not just JavaScript), readable pages, an llms.txt file, Open Graph share tags, and no accidental “keep out” signs.
