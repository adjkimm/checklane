Category: The AI checkout handshake

No UCP checkout file found

What it means: We checked the standard discovery paths (starting with the canonical /.well-known/ucp). Nothing there. This is the faster machine path for UCP-capable agents, not a prerequisite. Browser-driving agents buy through normal checkout today.

How to fix it: Publish your UCP capability profile at /.well-known/ucp. The file itself is hours; a real UCP implementation (working REST endpoints, payment handlers with signing keys, order webhooks) is days to weeks of backend engineering. A static JSON pointing at dead endpoints is worse than none.

What we look at

Your human shopper sees one assistant. Your site sees 28 agents: the major AI crawlers and agent fetchers, each reading your site its own way. Every check across five categories tests whether every one of them can find, read, and buy from you.

The AI checkout handshake

The behind-the-scenes file that tells AI shoppers “yes, you can buy here”: Google’s Universal Commerce Protocol profile (/.well-known/ucp) and Agentic Commerce Protocol (ACP) checkout signals. It’s the faster, more reliable machine path for agent checkout.

Product info AI can read

Clean, labeled details using schema.org Product markup: names, photos, prices, stock. AI reads labels first; unlabeled pages may as well be invisible.

Can AI read your pages?

The basics: real text (not just JavaScript), readable pages, an llms.txt file, Open Graph share tags, and no accidental “keep out” signs.

Common questions

What does my score tell me?

Whether AI shoppers can technically find, read, and buy from your site. It answers the urgent question first: can AI shoppers buy from you at all? Many sites struggle on basic technical grounds before products or prices ever matter.

Do you need my password or admin access?

Nope. We only read your public pages, the same ones any visitor sees. Nothing to install, no login.

Do I have to lower my defenses to let AI shoppers in?

No. The AI crawlers that read your site publish their addresses, so you can welcome each one by name while your firewall keeps blocking everything else. For purchases, checkout protocols like UCP and ACP let an AI shopper buy through a structured, authenticated connection, and your checkout keeps the fraud protections you already have.

My site isn’t a store. Is this for me?

Yes. Checklane grades any public website. Storefront-specific checks like product data are marked not applicable for non-storefronts; you’re scored on what matters for your site: AI bot access, readability, llms.txt, sitemap, meta tags, and FAQ content.

Will ChatGPT, Claude, Google, or Perplexity start recommending my products?

Recommendations are the AI’s call. But AI can only recommend stores it can read, and most stores are unreliably legible to AI shoppers right now. You can’t buy a recommendation, but you can remove every technical reason AI skips you.

Can’t I just ask ChatGPT or Claude to check this?

You can, but a chatbot only shows you one AI’s view. The visitor that can cost you sales is the one that gets blocked. It never gets far enough to tell you.
