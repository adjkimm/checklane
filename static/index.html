What we look at

The AI checkout handshake

The behind-the-scenes file that tells AI shoppers “yes, you can buy here.” Without it, they can’t even start.

Product info AI can read

Clean, labeled details — names, photos, prices, stock. AI reads labels first; unlabeled pages may as well be invisible.

Can AI find your products?

Your sitemap and product feeds. If AI can’t list your catalog, it can’t sell a thing.

Are you blocking AI visitors?

We visit your site as six popular AI shoppers. If your security turns them away, real AI shoppers bounce too.

Can AI read your pages?

The basics: real text (not just JavaScript), readable pages, and no accidental “keep out” signs.

Common questions

Does a good score guarantee sales?

No. We grade what’s technically true about your store — things we can actually check. We don’t certify your business, and nobody can promise what an AI will recommend.

Do you need my password or admin access?

Nope. We only read your public pages — the same ones any visitor sees. Nothing to install, no login.

Will this make ChatGPT recommend my products?

Nobody can promise that. What we measure is whether AI can find, read, and buy from your store. Being findable is step one — necessary, but not a guarantee.

What does the founding beta include?

Ongoing checks that your products still show up for AI shoppers, alerts when AI visits your store, and a Checklane badge showing the world you’re AI-ready.
