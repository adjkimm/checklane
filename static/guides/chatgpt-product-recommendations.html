Why ChatGPT Isn't Recommending Your Products

No technical background required. About a 4-minute read.

Type "where should I buy handmade leather wallets" into ChatGPT and you get a tidy list of stores. If you sell handmade leather wallets and your store never appears, it is not a coincidence and it is not personal. The AI recommended the stores it could read. Yours may not be one of them.

How ChatGPT picks products to recommend

When someone asks ChatGPT a shopping question, it does not browse the way you do. It pulls from what it has read across the web: product pages, reviews, structured product data, and merchant listings. Then it assembles an answer from the stores it understood best.

That means the recommendation is downstream of readability. A store with clear product names, real prices in the page code, and pages that load as plain text is easy to include. A store where the price only exists inside an image, or where product details are buried in a script the AI cannot run, is easy to skip.

"You cannot buy a recommendation. You can only remove every technical reason the AI skips you."

The usual reasons your store gets skipped

Your product pages have no structured data. Structured data is labeled product information in your page code: name, price, availability, image. Without it, the AI has to guess what is a price and what is a headline. Guessing loses to certainty every time.

Key details only exist in images. If your price, sizes, or options are baked into product photos, text-reading AI cannot see them. The information might as well not be there.

You are blocking AI crawlers. Some sites block GPTBot or other AI crawlers in robots.txt, often without realizing it. Blocked means unreadable, and unreadable means unrecommendable.

Your pages are thin. A product page with a title, a price, and nothing else gives the AI almost nothing to quote. Pages with real descriptions, specs, and details get cited because there is something to cite.

What you can actually control

Run through this list before anything else:

1. Add Product structured data to every product page (name, price, availability, image).
2. Make sure prices, options, and key details exist as real text, not just images.
3. Check your robots.txt and confirm you are not blocking GPTBot, ClaudeBot, or PerplexityBot.
4. Write product descriptions a human would find useful. AI quotes useful pages.
5. Add an llms.txt file that summarizes your business for AI readers.

What you cannot control

Even a perfectly readable store is not guaranteed a recommendation. The AI weighs reviews, reputation, price, shipping, and its own opaque ranking. Readability gets you into the candidate pool. Everything after that is the AI's call, and nobody outside the AI companies knows the exact formula. Anyone who promises you a top recommendation is selling something.

Start with what is measurable

You cannot see ChatGPT's ranking formula, but you can see exactly how readable your site is to AI. That is the part you control, and it is the part most small stores are failing. Check yours before you assume the problem is your products.
