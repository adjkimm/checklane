llms.txt Explained: The Small File That Tells AI About Your Business

No technical background required. About a 4-minute read.

Your website has a robots.txt that talks to search engines. Now there is a newer convention: an llms.txt file that talks to AI systems. It is a short plain-text summary of your business, sitting at yoursite.com/llms.txt, written for machines that read. If AI shoppers are going to understand your store, this file is one of the cheapest ways to help them.

What llms.txt actually is

It is a Markdown file at the root of your website. It answers the questions an AI would ask before recommending you: what do you sell, who is it for, where do you ship, how does buying work. Instead of making the AI piece that together from dozens of pages, you hand it the summary directly.

The convention was proposed as a standard the way robots.txt and sitemap.xml were: a file in a known location, in a predictable format, that machines check first. Adoption is still early, but the cost of adding one is near zero.

"llms.txt is your elevator pitch, filed where AI looks first."

What to put in it

Keep it short and factual. A good llms.txt for a store has:

Who you are. Business name, what you sell, who it is for, in one or two sentences.
What you sell. Your main product categories, with links to the category pages.
How buying works. Where you ship, returns policy in one line, how checkout works.
Key pages. Links to your bestsellers, shipping info, and contact page.
What is new. Anything timely: seasonal lines, current promotions, restocks.

Do not stuff it with keywords or marketing fluff. AI systems are good at ignoring fluff and good at using facts. Give them facts.

An example

# Acme Outdoor Gear We sell durable hiking and camping gear for weekend backpackers. Ships across the US. 60-day returns, no questions asked. ## Products - Backpacks: https://example.com/collections/backpacks - Tents: https://example.com/collections/tents - Sale: https://example.com/collections/sale ## Policies - Shipping: https://example.com/shipping - Returns: https://example.com/returns

How to add it to your site

1. Write the file in any text editor and save it as llms.txt.
2. Upload it to the root of your website, so it loads at yoursite.com/llms.txt.
3. On Shopify: add it as a page template redirect, or serve it through an app or your theme files. There is no one-click upload, but any developer can place it in under an hour.
4. Visit the URL yourself to confirm it loads as plain text.

What llms.txt will not do

It will not make AI recommend you on its own, and it is not a ranking trick. Think of it as removing friction: when an AI is deciding between your store and a competitor it understands equally well on product, the store that handed it a clear summary is easier to cite with confidence. It is one input among many, and a cheap one.
