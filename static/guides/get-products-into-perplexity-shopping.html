How to Get Your Products Into Perplexity's Shopping Answers

Practical steps, no developer required for most. About a 4-minute read.

When someone asks Perplexity "what is the best X for Y," it searches the live web and builds an answer from what it finds, with sources shown. Products get named when they are easy to find, easy to understand, and backed by proof. Here is how to make yours one of them.

Step 1: Let PerplexityBot in

Perplexity's crawler is called PerplexityBot. Open yoursite.com/robots.txt and check for it next to the word Disallow. If it is blocked, you are shutting Perplexity out of your own pages, and no other step can make up for that.

Step 2: Make each product page self-contained

Perplexity retrieves passages, not whole sites. One product page should carry everything on its own: the product name as the main heading, the price in plain text, a short direct description, key facts as a list, availability in words, and shipping and returns in plain sentences. If a fact lives three clicks away, assume it will not be found.

Step 3: Add Product structured data

Label the facts with schema.org Product markup: name, price, currency, availability. This removes guesswork for any machine reading the page. Clear visible copy comes first; structured data reinforces it.

Step 4: Earn proof off your site

Perplexity tends to weigh reviews, press, and community discussion heavily in shopping answers. Ask customers for reviews and make it easy: a short link in the order confirmation works. A genuine mention of your product in a relevant community thread can keep surfacing in answers for months.

Step 5: Publish what shoppers actually ask

"Best X for Y" guides and honest comparisons in your category are the pages Perplexity cites when answering buying questions. Write the guide you wish existed when you were learning your own market. Answer first, sell second.

"Perplexity names the products it can find, understand, and verify. Be all three."

What will not help

There is no merchant feed to submit and no paid placement inside the answer. Shortcuts do not exist here. The way in is being findable, readable, and vouched for, which is also exactly what helps you everywhere else AI answers questions.
