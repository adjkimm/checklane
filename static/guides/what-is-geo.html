What Is GEO?

No jargon, we promise. About a 3-minute read.

GEO stands for generative engine optimization. Plain version: it is the work of making your website easy for AI assistants like ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google's AI answers to read, understand, and mention. If SEO was about ranking in a list of links, GEO is about being included in the answer itself.

How GEO differs from SEO

SEO asks: how do I rank higher? GEO asks: would an AI confidently quote my page? The two overlap. Strong SEO helps, because AI tools still pull heavily from search indexes. But a page can rank on page one and never be quoted, because ranking rewards the whole page while AI lifts a short passage that directly answers the question.

Think of it this way. SEO wins the click. GEO wins the mention. Shoppers are increasingly skipping the click and acting on the mention.

What actually matters

Five things, in rough order:

1. Let AI crawlers in. If GPTBot, ClaudeBot, and PerplexityBot cannot read your pages, you are not in the running.

2. Answer questions directly. Put the direct answer near the top of the page, in plain words. AI lifts passages, not whole pages.

3. State facts plainly. What you sell, what it costs, where you operate, how to reach you. No slogans where facts should be.

4. Label facts with structured data. Schema markup tells machines exactly which number is the price and which line is the address, so nothing is guessed.

5. Earn mentions elsewhere. Reviews, press, community discussion. AI trusts what others confirm.

What matters less than people claim

There is no secret trick. A llms.txt file on its own will not make you appear in answers; it is one small signal among many. Stuffing keywords "for AI" does not work either. The fundamentals are boring: access, clarity, proof.

"SEO asks: how do I rank higher? GEO asks: would an AI confidently quote my page?"

Do you need GEO if you already rank well?

Ranking well is a head start, not a guarantee. The check is simple: ask an AI assistant the questions your customers ask. If it does not mention you, your ranking did not carry over, and the five items above are your to-do list.
