What the audit found

  • No sitemap. There's no map of the site for machine visitors to list what's on it. Basic plumbing, now fixed.
  • No structured data. The pages had no machine-readable labels — AI had to guess what everything was. That's next on our fix list.
  • Missing canonical link. Pages didn't name their canonical URL — the kind of small "keep out" sign a confused AI reader trips over. Fixed.

The good news, and it's real: everything dragging the score down is plumbing, not strategy. No rebrand, no new products — a few hours of focused technical work.

One honest caveat

We're a marketing site, not a storefront — and two of the five categories assume products, prices, and a cart. That part of the F is an artifact of the scoring, not of our site, so we built site-type-aware scoring before publishing an "after" number. We'd rather fix the ruler than celebrate with a bent one. Before this page goes live, we'll re-audit on the current engine and publish the real before → after.
